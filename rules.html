Rules Summary (Beyblade X)

⚠️ This is a general summary only. The official rules provided by the event organizer take precedence over anything on this page.

Decks

Each blader brings a 3-on-3 deck: three Beyblades used across a match, generally without repeating parts between them.

Scoring

  • Spin Finish: opponent’s bey stops spinning first, typically 1 point.
  • Over Finish: opponent’s bey is knocked into an over zone, typically 2 points.
  • Burst Finish: opponent’s bey comes apart, typically 2 points.
  • Xtreme Finish: opponent’s bey is knocked into the Xtreme zone, typically 3 points.

Matches are usually played to 4 points; first blader to reach 4 wins.

Launching

  • Both bladers launch together on the countdown (“3, 2, 1, Go Shoot!”).
  • Launch from above the stadium without touching it; mistimed or faulty launches may be relaunched or penalized per the official rules.
  • Don’t touch beys or the stadium until the battle ends.

Full details: see the official Takara Tomy Beyblade X site, the World Beyblade Organization, and your organizer’s rules.
